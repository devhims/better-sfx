# Messages

- `message-send` (0.12s). A short upward whoosh for a sent message.
- `message-receive` (0.52s). Two marimba notes for an incoming message.
- `message-pop` (0.36s). A chat-bubble pop with a small chime.
- `glass-send` (0.36s). A quick rush up that ends in a tap with bright, airy glass.
- `wood-send` (0.145s). A quick rush up that ends in a tap with warm, dry wood.
- `soft-send` (0.135s). A quick rush up that ends in a tap with soft, rounded tones.
- `retro-send` (0.12s). A quick rush up that ends in a tap with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `mechanical-send` (0.125s). A quick rush up that ends in a tap with a sharp metallic edge.
