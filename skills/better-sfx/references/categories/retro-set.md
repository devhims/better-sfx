# Retro set

- `retro-click` (0.02s). A button press with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-hover` (0.02s). A faint hover or focus cue with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-toggle` (0.215s). Two rising notes for a switch with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-notify` (0.76s). A two-note notification with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-success` (0.77s). A major arpeggio up to the octave with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-error` (0.435s). Two notes falling a tritone with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-open` (0.31s). An airy upward sweep and two rising notes with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-close` (0.31s). An airy downward sweep and two falling notes with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-send` (0.12s). A quick rush up that ends in a tap with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-delete` (0.32s). A tap that drops into two low notes with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
