# Clicks

- `click-soft` (0.025s). A quiet, rounded click for everyday buttons.
- `click-crisp` (0.015s). A short, bright click with a hard edge.
- `click-deep` (0.045s). A low, weighty click with a soft thump underneath.
- `button-press` (0.1s). A tactile button that goes down, then springs back up.
- `tap-wood` (0.07s). A dry tap on a small wooden block.
- `tap-glass` (0.155s). A light fingernail tap on glass.
- `tick-tiny` (0.01s). The smallest possible tick, for selection and focus.
- `hover-blip` (0.035s). A faint blip for hover and focus states.
- `glass-click` (0.06s). A button press with bright, airy glass.
- `glass-hover` (0.065s). A faint hover or focus cue with bright, airy glass.
- `wood-click` (0.045s). A button press with warm, dry wood.
- `wood-hover` (0.045s). A faint hover or focus cue with warm, dry wood.
- `soft-click` (0.035s). A button press with soft, rounded tones.
- `soft-hover` (0.035s). A faint hover or focus cue with soft, rounded tones.
- `retro-click` (0.02s). A button press with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `retro-hover` (0.02s). A faint hover or focus cue with a buzzy 8-bit tone.
- `mechanical-click` (0.025s). A button press with a sharp metallic edge.
- `mechanical-hover` (0.03s). A faint hover or focus cue with a sharp metallic edge.
