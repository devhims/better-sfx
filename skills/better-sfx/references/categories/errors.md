# Errors

- `error-buzz` (0.225s). A low buzzer for something that failed.
- `error-double` (0.37s). Two falling notes that sound like no.
- `error-thud` (0.16s). A dull thud for a blocked action.
- `warning-beep` (0.345s). Two plain 1 kHz beeps.
- `denied-wobble` (0.27s). A wobbly low note that shakes its head.
- `invalid-input` (0.145s). A soft two-tap shake for a bad form field.
- `glass-error` (0.595s). Two notes falling a tritone. Part of the glass set.
- `wood-error` (0.47s). Two notes falling a tritone. Part of the wood set.
- `soft-error` (0.645s). Two notes falling a tritone. Part of the soft set.
- `retro-error` (0.435s). Two notes falling a tritone. Part of the retro set.
- `mechanical-error` (0.38s). Two notes falling a tritone. Part of the mechanical set.
